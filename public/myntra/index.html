Myntra Price Alerts

Send a Myntra product link to a Telegram bot, pick your size and the price you want to pay. It watches the listing and messages you when your size is in stock at or below your price.

Open the bot in Telegram

Free while in beta.

How it works

  1. Open a product in the Myntra app or site, tap Share, and send the link to the bot.
  2. Tap your size. The bot shows the current price for every size.
  3. Send the price you want to pay, and choose how often it should check.

What it watches for

  • Your size, not the product. A shoe can be ₹2,000 in size 10 and ₹2,600 in size 8. The bot tracks the size you asked for.
  • The cheapest seller in stock. Several sellers often list the same item at different prices.
  • Back in stock. If your size is sold out, the alert waits until it returns at your price.
  • One message per drop. It alerts when your price is reached, then only again if the price falls further.

Commands

  • /list — what the bot is watching for you, with a button to stop any of them
  • /history — trackers that expired or were stopped, to renew or delete
  • /help — how it all works

Affiliate disclosure

When the bot tells you about a price drop, the "Buy on Myntra" button may be an affiliate link. If you buy through it, this service may earn a commission from the retailer at no extra cost to you. It never changes the price you pay, and it never changes which items get tracked or when an alert is sent — alerts are based only on the price and size you asked for.

Privacy

The bot stores only what it needs to send your alerts: your Telegram chat ID and name, the products, sizes and target prices you asked it to watch, and the prices it has seen for those products.

  • No payment details are collected or stored.
  • Your data is not sold, and is not shared with anyone except as needed to deliver messages to you through Telegram.
  • Stopping a tracker removes it from your list, and old trackers are deleted 30 days after they end.
  • To have your data removed, send /history to the bot and delete your trackers, or message the bot to ask.
