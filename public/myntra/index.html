Why sizes matter more than prices

A Myntra listing isn't one price. Several sellers stock the same item, each with their own price and their own sizes, and the number on the page is only one of them.

Your size, its own price

The same shoe can be ₹2,087 in size 10 and ₹2,600 in size 8. This bot watches the size you actually wear.

It compares every seller holding your size and uses the lowest price you could actually check out at.

Managing what you're watching

/list — everything you're watching, with the current price and what you're waiting for. Extend one for longer, or stop it.

/history — trackers that expired or you stopped. Renew one in a tap, or delete it.

Extending early never costs you time — the new days are added to what's left, not counted from today.

You'll also get a message a day before a tracker ends, with a button to extend it, so nothing stops quietly.

/help — the whole thing again, in the chat.

Earn free trackers

Every alert carries a link to the product. If you buy through it, you get another free tracker.

That link is an affiliate link: if you buy through it, this service earns a commission from the retailer, at no extra cost to you and with no change to the price you pay. As a thank-you, each purchase through one of my links banks one extra free tracker for you, up to ten.

Purchases are confirmed by the retailer after its returns window, so a banked tracker can take a few weeks to appear. Returned orders don't count.

Questions

Is it really free?

The free plan is free with no card and no signup: one tracker at a time, checked every 12 hours, for 7 days. Faster plans cost 5 or 10 Telegram Stars, and buying through an alert link earns you free trackers instead.

What are Telegram Stars?

Telegram's own currency for paying inside bots, bought in the Telegram app with UPI, Google Pay or Apple Pay. Roughly ₹1 per Star, in bundles. Telegram requires bots to use Stars for digital services, which is why the faster plans are priced that way rather than in rupees.

Do you make money from this?

The "Buy on Myntra" button in an alert may be an affiliate link, which can earn a commission from the retailer if you buy through it. It costs you nothing extra and never changes the price you pay. It also has no bearing on which items get tracked or when an alert fires — those depend only on the size and price you asked for.

What do you store about me?

Your Telegram chat ID and name, the products, sizes and target prices you asked for, and the prices seen for those products. No payment details, ever. Nothing is sold or shared beyond delivering your messages through Telegram. Stop a tracker and it leaves your list; old trackers are deleted 30 days after they end.

Is this an official Myntra service?

No. It's an independent tool, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Myntra. It reads the same public product page you'd see in your browser.

How fast will I hear about a drop?

Within your plan's check window — up to 12 hours on the free plan, 4–6 hours on the ₹10 plan. Prices on Myntra can change again at any moment, so check the listing before buying.

Why Telegram and not WhatsApp or email?

Telegram bots are free to run and need no signup, which is why the beta is free. WhatsApp charges per message, so it may come later.

Can I track something without a price in mind?

Yes. When it asks for your target price, tap "Just tell me when it's in stock" and you'll hear the moment your size is available again, whatever it costs.

How do I stop everything?

Send /list and tap Stop on each tracker, or just block the bot — it stops watching and messaging you immediately.
